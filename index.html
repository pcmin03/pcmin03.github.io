About me

I bring vision AI onto the steel-plant floor, from unmanned cranes to the jump from smart factory to intelligent factory.

I am a Spatial Intelligence Vision Engineer in the AX Technology R&D Group at POSCO DX. My main work is crane automation: perception, sim-to-real training, and control integration that let industrial cranes run without an operator.

Around that I build the factory-side AI stack, including defect detection, retrieval, MLOps, and L2/PLC integration. Before POSCO DX I spent three years at VUNO taking vision models from research to certified products.

Crane Automation

Vision AI that perceives cargo, holds, and surroundings so cranes can unload and move material without an operator.

Smart → Intelligent Factory

Apply AI on the production line itself: inspection, retrieval, and continuous training wired into plant control.

Physical AI · Sim2Real

Train in Isaac Sim, fuse LiDAR, ToF, and RGB, and transfer models that hold up under real site conditions.
